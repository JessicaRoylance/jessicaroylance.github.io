Jessica Roylance

I studied English at Brigham Young University, with a minor in Computers and the Humanities. I worked in computer labs and helped with application projects.

Later, I worked as a technical writer. It gave me years of experience exploring software, testing workflows, and explaining how things work. It also strengthened my ability to "question everything," think critically, and pay close attention to details.

Eventually, I wanted to go beyond explaining technology and learn to build it. I earned my B.S. in Computer Science from Western Governors University in 2026 and now work as an Application Developer at Cincinnati Children's.

What keeps me curious

  • Making work easier. Reducing repetitive tasks and simplifying processes.
  • Understanding the details. Asking questions and figuring out why things work the way they do.
  • Thoughtful design. Creating clear, consistent, intuitive experiences.
  • Learning something new. Exploring tools, concepts, and problems that are new to me.

I still have so much to learn!
